## 1. Permitted Source Classes
1. `curated_human`: Authored directly by research team.
2. `verified_corpus`: Extracted from verified Phase 55 corpus with permissive rights.
3. `controlled_synthetic`: Generated via deterministic procedural rule templates, labeled explicitly.
4. `rule_transformation`: Grammatical, script, or linguistic transformations with logged provenance.
