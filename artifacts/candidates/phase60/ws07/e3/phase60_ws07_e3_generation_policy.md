## 1. Seven Expansion Modes
1. **Word-Level:** Vocabulary alignment (`அம்மா` -> `mother` -> `amma`).
2. **Phrase-Level:** Common idiomatic phrases (`என் அம்மா` -> `my mother` -> `en amma`).
3. **Sentence-Level:** Complete grammatical sentences.
4. **Translation-Direction:** Bidirectional tasks (`ta->en`, `en->ta`, `ta->tgl`, `tgl->ta`).
5. **Mixed-Bilingual:** Code-switched sentences reflecting real user colloquial speech.
6. **Conversational QA:** Two-turn prompt/response exchanges.
7. **Instruction-Following:** Direct imperative commands.

## 2. Volume Bounds
- Max 8 proposals generated per source concept to prevent synthetic data explosion.
